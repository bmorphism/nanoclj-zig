\section*{1. The 4$\times$2 cell lattice}

Each Clojure runtime's \texttt{visible-width} is a single point in
$\{\text{regex}, \text{scanner}\} \times \{\text{byte}, \text{u16}, \text{cp}, \text{graph}\}$.
Seven of eight cells are occupied; the (\textsf{scanner}, u16) cell is structurally
empty because no runtime hand-walks UTF-16 surrogates while doing OSC 8 scanning.
Diagonal dotted arrows are the ``forgetful refinement'' maps from finer to coarser counts.

\[
\xymatrix@C=4em@R=4em{
                 & \text{byte}
                 & \text{u16}
                 & \text{cp}
                 & \text{grapheme} \\
  \text{regex}  &
       *+[F]{D}\ar[r]\ar@{.>}[rr]\ar@{.>}[rrr]
       &
       *+[F]{B}\ar[r]\ar@{.>}[rr]
       &
       *+[F]{A}\ar[r] &
       *+[F]{G} \\
  \text{scanner}&
       *+[F]{E}\ar[uu]\ar@{.>}[rr]\ar@{.>}[rrr]
       &
       *++[o][F.]{\bigotimes}
       &
       *+[F]{C}\ar[uu]\ar[r] &
       *+[F]{H}\ar[uu]
}
\]

The \texttt{$\bigotimes$} marker is the structurally-empty cell.
Vertical arrows are ``swap strip mode''; horizontal/diagonal arrows are
``coarsen the count.'' All paths from \emph{D} (lower-left) to \emph{H}
(upper-right) commute up to natural isomorphism.

\section*{2. Cross-runtime evaluation as a 2-categorical pyramid}

Source code is parsed identically in every Clojureme; evaluation diverges
into per-runtime functors $\mathsf{eval}_X$ producing values in
profile-tagged $\mathbb{N}$. The horizontal natural transformations
$T_{AB}$ are the cross-runtime translations; the vertical $\Downarrow\alpha$
2-cells witness commutativity at the \emph{value} layer.

\[
\xymatrix@C=4em@R=3em{
   & & \mathbf{Source}\ar[dll]_{\mathsf{parse}}\ar[d]^{\mathsf{parse}}\ar[drr]^{\mathsf{parse}} & & \\
   \mathbf{AST}_A\ar[d]_{\mathsf{eval}_A}
       \ar@/^1pc/[rr]^{T_{AB}}
   & & \mathbf{AST}_B\ar[d]^{\mathsf{eval}_B}
       \ar@/^1pc/[rr]^{T_{BC}}
   & & \mathbf{AST}_C\ar[d]^{\mathsf{eval}_C} \\
   \mathbb{N}_{(\textsf{regex},\textsf{cp})}
       \ar[d]_{\pi_A}
   & & \mathbb{N}_{(\textsf{regex},\textsf{u16})}
       \ar@{}[ull]|{\Downarrow\alpha}
       \ar[d]^{\pi_B}
   & & \mathbb{N}_{(\textsf{scanner},\textsf{byte})}
       \ar@{}[ull]|{\Downarrow\beta}
       \ar[d]^{\pi_C} \\
   & & \mathbb{N}\;(\text{abstract value})
       \ar@/_2pc/[uull]_{\Sigma_A}
       \ar@/^2pc/[uurr]^{\Sigma_C}
   & &
}
\]

\section*{4. The falsification cascade as a spectral sequence}

The Galois rank we could prove from each corpus snapshot increased monotonically.
Each ``page'' $E_r$ is a corpus, each differential is the falsifying
counter-example set that forces a basis extension.

\[
\xymatrix@C=3em@R=3em{
*+[F]{E_0:\text{ joker}\atop\text{13 cases}}
   \ar[r]^{d_0}_{\text{adversarial 60+}}
& *+[F]{E_1:\text{ corpus}\atop\text{77 cases}}
   \ar[r]^{d_1}_{\text{K1\ldots K5}}
& *+[F]{E_2:\text{ extended}\atop\text{82 cases}}
   \ar@{.>}[r]^{d_2}_{?}
& *+[F.]{E_3 = ?} \\
\text{rank} = 1
   \ar@{=>}[u]
& \text{rank} = 2
   \ar@{=>}[u]
& \text{rank} = 3
   \ar@{=>}[u]
& \text{rank} \geq 4 ?
   \ar@{=>}[u] \\
\text{basis} = \{A\}
& \text{basis} = \{A,E\}
& \text{basis} = \{A,E,G\}
& \text{basis} = \{A,E,G,?\}
}
\]

The differentials carry the structural axes that each extension forced
into the basis: $d_0$ adds the strip and byte axes; $d_1$ adds the
grapheme axis; $d_2$ would add (e.g.) East-Asian-Wide or RTL embedding axes
not yet exercised.

\section*{5. The (lookup $\dashv$ forget) adjunction}

The 3-basis $(A, E, G)$ is left-adjoint to the forgetful map that
projects a 7-tuple onto its three basis coordinates. The lookup is
idempotent under composition: forgetting then re-deriving recovers
the original 7-tuple.

\[
\xymatrix@C=10em@R=2em{
  *+[F.]{(A,E,G) \in \mathbb{N}^3}
  \ar@/^1.5pc/[r]^{F:\text{ lookup}}
  &
  *+[F.]{(A,B,C,D,E,G,H) \in \mathbb{N}^7}
    \ar@/^1.5pc/[l]^{U:\text{ forget B,C,D,H}}_{\dashv}
}
\]

\[
\xymatrix@C=4em@R=2.5em{
  & *++[F]{\text{Source AST}}
    \ar[dl]_{\mathsf{parse}_A}
    \ar[d]_{\mathsf{parse}_E}
    \ar[dr]^{\mathsf{parse}_G} & \\
  *+[F]{\mathsf{eval}_A}\ar[dr]_{\pi_A}
  & *+[F]{\mathsf{eval}_E}\ar[d]_{\pi_E}
  & *+[F]{\mathsf{eval}_G}\ar[dl]^{\pi_G} \\
  & *++[o][F]{F(A,E,G)\;=\;\text{full 7-tuple}}
    \ar@(dl,dr)[]_{\text{idempotent}}
}
\]

\noindent\textbf{Take-away.} The whole session compresses to
$2\times 4 = 8$ cells (one vacant), three rank-pages of falsification,
and a single adjunction between $\mathbb{N}^3$ and $\mathbb{N}^7$.
